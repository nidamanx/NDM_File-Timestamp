INSTRUCTIONS - delete this whole box before signing
================================================================================
Template of a declaration of authorship of a work, to be used together with the
proofs made by NDM File Timestamp (https://github.com/nidamanx/NDM_File-Timestamp).

1. Stamp the work first:        ./ndm_file-timestamp.sh work.zip
   then verify it:              ./ndm_file-timestamp.sh --verify work.zip
   From the --verify output, copy the SHA-256 and the "at least since" date.
2. Copy this template to a folder of the work, for example
   ndm_file-timestamp-attachments/work/, and work on the copy.
   Replace every [PLACEHOLDER] with your details; delete the lines and the
   alternatives you do not need (those starting with "or").
3. Sign the declaration:
   - by hand: print it, sign it, scan it to PDF;
   - better: with a digital signature (in the EU: a qualified electronic
     signature).
4. Stamp the signed declaration too:
                                ./ndm_file-timestamp.sh declaration.pdf
   For stronger protection, also register or deposit it, together with the
   work, with the body of your country (copyright office, notary; in Italy a
   PEC or SIAE).
5. Keep everything together in one archive (work, declaration, receipts and
   their proofs). Keep only the final documents in the folder of the work:
     ./ndm_file-timestamp.sh --archive work.zip \
         --attach ndm_file-timestamp-attachments/work/
6. Keep the archive in at least two places. Do not open and save the stamped
   files again: a single different byte invalidates the proofs. After every
   copy, check the copy with --verify.

The declaration alone does not prove authorship: it is a signed statement,
which gains weight together with drafts, working files and dated proofs.
This template is generic and is not legal advice.
================================================================================
